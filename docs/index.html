How it works: each note checks the official close every two weeks for up to a year. At or above the start price, it ends early and pays back. At or above 65% of the start, it pays a coupon. Only a final close below 65% "knocks in", paying the cash side in stock at the start price. Returns use the docs' illustrative 150 bps coupon per check, net of the 15% fee. Prices are Yahoo Finance closes adjusted for splits and dividends. Past results are hypothetical and not a forecast; nothing here is financial advice.

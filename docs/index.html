Say you put $1,000 into a note. What would have happened?

How the note works, in three lines. You lend $1,000 against one stock for up to a year.
  1. Every two weeks, if the stock hasn't fallen more than 35%, you get paid $12.75.
  2. If the stock is back at or above where it started, the note ends early and you get your $1,000 back.
  3. If the stock is down more than 35% at the very end, you're paid in the stock instead of cash. That's the risk you're being paid for.

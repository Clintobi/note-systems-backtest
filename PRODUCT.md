# Product

**Register:** brand (shareable explainer; the design is the product)

## What it is

A one-page interactive simulator at clintobi.github.io/note-systems-backtest. You pick one of the eight Stock Tokens on Note Systems testnet and a start week between 2015 and 2025, and it shows what $1,000 in the default autocallable note would have done: every fortnightly check, each coupon, how the note ended, and how that compares with just holding the stock. The data is a real-price backtest (3,683 notes) from this repo.

## Who it's for

- Crypto-curious readers who reach it from the @0xbuchi X article *Structured Notes, But on the Blockchain*, usually on a phone. Most have never heard of a structured note.
- The Note Systems team, who are judging the writing contest it supports.

## Job to be done

Within 30 seconds a newcomer should get three things: what a note pays, how it usually ends (early, within weeks), and what the rare bad ending looks like. Once they've got it, they should want to share a specific note they found.

## Personality

Calm and trustworthy. It should feel like a well-kept bank passbook or a clear statement: plain, confident, precise with numbers. The money story supplies the drama, so the interface doesn't need to.

## Anti-references

- Generic crypto dashboards (neon on black, glow, degen trading UI)
- AI-template landing pages (hero metric plus card grid, gradient text)
- The article's dark charcoal chart style; this page has its own identity

## Constraints

- Static page on GitHub Pages; the only data is docs/notes.json
- No em dashes or en dashes in the copy
- Neutral, fair tone toward Note Systems; independent work, not affiliated
- Accessible: 4.5:1 contrast, keyboard operable, reduced-motion support
